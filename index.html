Few words about me

My name is FAHIM NOOR BHATTI. I'm a 36 year old 3D & 2D Artist from Karachi, Pakistan .I love all things technology, and always try to keep up with the latest gadgets. My other hobbies include: photography, gaming, sketching, reading, going to seaview,. Music is a huge part of my life, and I listen to a variety of different styles.

What I do

I studied Computer Animation for three years at the Arena Multimedia and I'm currently working with automation company . I am currently available for freelance work and you can check out my previous work here.


I have had an interest in animation ever since seeing Animated movies for the first time as a child. I'm highly self-motivated, and am constantly working on new ideas. My strengths are in 3D Visualization, and I love working on interior designs in particular. I have a true passion for what
I do and I'm always trying to challenge myself.


I love to create high quality stills and enjoy the challenge of making them as realistic as possible. Throughout university we learned 3d MAx and used Photoshop for compositing. However since leaving, I have taught myself many more 3D programs, listed to the left. I mainly use to create my work. I am a fast learner and enjoy the challenge of learning something new. As well as providing 3D services, I am also able to do sketches, concept art, storyboards, visual effects and compositing. Feel free to contact me regarding any of the services I offer.
